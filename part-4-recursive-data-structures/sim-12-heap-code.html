Heap Insertion — SiftUp

Key Idea: Appending to the end maintains the complete binary tree shape. Then sifting up restores the heap order property by bubbling the new element to its correct position. At most O(log n) swaps.

  • Append: Place the new value at the next available position (end of array).
  • Compare with parent: If the new value is smaller than its parent, swap them.
  • Repeat: Continue comparing and swapping upward until the heap property holds or the root is reached.
  • Time complexity: O(log n) — at most one swap per level.
