Why This Matters

Tree Explosion: Without memoization, fib(n) makes ~1.618ⁿ calls. Each node calls two children!

n=4: 9 calls → n=6: 25 calls → n=10: 177 calls!

Memoization Prunes: Repeated subproblems turn green instantly - no re-computation needed.

Complexity: O(2ⁿ) → O(n) with memoization. That's the power of caching!

Real Analogy: Like remembering answers to math problems instead of solving them repeatedly.
