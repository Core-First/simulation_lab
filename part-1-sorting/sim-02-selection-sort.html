Algorithm Information

Selection Sort divides the input list into two parts: a sorted sublist of items which is built up from left to right at the front, and a sublist of the remaining unsorted items. The algorithm repeatedly finds the smallest element from the unsorted sublist and swaps it with the leftmost unsorted element.

❌ Unstable ✅ In-Place ❌ Non-Adaptive
Best: O(n²) Avg: O(n²) Worst: O(n²) Space: O(1)
