Algorithm Information

Quick Sort is a highly efficient, comparison-based sorting algorithm that uses a divide-and-conquer strategy. It selects a 'pivot' element and partitions the other elements into two sub-arrays according to whether they are less than or greater than the pivot. The sub-arrays are then sorted recursively.

❌ Unstable ✅ In-Place ❌ Non-Adaptive
Best: O(n log n) Avg: O(n log n) Worst: O(n²) Space: O(log n)
